ABOUT THE PROJECT

SmartCrop AI

SmartCrop AI is an academic prototype that demonstrates a supervised Machine Learning workflow for crop-class recommendation from selected soil and climate attributes.

Input attributes

  • Nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium
  • Temperature and relative humidity
  • Soil pH and rainfall

Limitations

The model learns patterns from a public educational dataset. It is not a certified agronomic advisory system and should not replace soil testing or advice from local agricultural experts.
